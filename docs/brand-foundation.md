# Facthor8 Brand and Commercial Foundation

## Brand core

**Name:** Facthor8

**Claim:** The Human Security Layer

**Category:** Human Risk Management
**Purpose:** Transform the human factor into an active, measurable and resilient line of cyber defense.

## The problem

Organizations invest heavily in technical controls, but everyday human decisions still create material exposure. Traditional annual awareness training proves attendance, not behavioral change. Facthor8 closes that gap through evidence, controlled practice, segmented learning and continuous measurement.

## Value proposition

Facthor8 helps organizations understand, reduce and demonstrate their human cyber risk. It combines cybersecurity judgment, behavioral principles and executive reporting in programs designed around each organization's real context.

## Flagship offer

### Cyber Culture Transformation Program

A minimum six-month engagement with four linked movements:

1. **Diagnose:** maturity, exposure, behavior and organizational context.
2. **Simulate:** controlled phishing and social-engineering scenarios.
3. **Transform:** concise, continuous, role-specific behavioral learning.
4. **Measure:** Human Cyber Risk Score, evidence, executive reporting and roadmap.

## Modular services

- Human Risk Assessment
- Controlled Phishing & Social Engineering Simulations
- Executive Cyber Readiness
- ISO 27001 Human-Factor Readiness
- Behavioral Training and Continuous Reinforcement
- Human Cyber Risk Score and Executive Reporting

## Primary deliverables

- Initial maturity and exposure baseline
- Human-risk map by role, area and behavior
- Controlled simulation plans and results
- Segmented learning plan
- Human Cyber Risk Score (HCRS)
- Executive progress reports
- Audit-ready evidence package
- Prioritized improvement roadmap

## Initial market

- Organizations with 20–500 employees
- Spain and Latin America
- Hybrid or distributed teams
- Regulated and trust-sensitive sectors
- Decision makers: CEO, CIO, CISO, IT Manager, HR/People, Compliance and Risk

## Tone of voice

- Precise, calm and evidence-led
- Human without being casual
- Strategic rather than alarmist
- Clear about outcomes; restrained about hype
- Never blame people or call them “the weakest link”

## Visual direction

Premium strategic cybersecurity: midnight navy, human green, mineral white, editorial typography, visible measurement systems and restrained motion. The identity should feel intelligent, rigorous and contemporary rather than aggressive or stereotypically “hacker”.
